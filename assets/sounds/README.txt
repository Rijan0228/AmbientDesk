AMBIENTDESK AUDIO FOLDER
========================

This folder is reserved for optional audio files.

AmbientDesk is intended to let users choose their own music.

No copyrighted music is bundled with the project.

The current music player expects these example filenames:

ambient-01.mp3
ambient-02.mp3
ambient-03.mp3

These are placeholders until the music player is updated to support
user-selected local audio files.

Do not upload music unless you have the right to use and distribute it.
